# Used campervans in Poland: scoring reference

Score each spec 0-10 and interpolate between bands. Info missing from the listing = max 3, and add it to "ask seller". (verify) = legal or uncertain; never state it as fact.

## Year
- 10: 2018+ | 7: 2013-2017 | 5: 2009-2012 | 3: 2004-2008 | 0: older, no documented restoration
- watch: year doesn't fit the engine (2143 cm3 only exists from 2009); Euro 4 or older restricted in some low-emission zones (verify)

## Mileage
- 10: <120k km | 7: 120-200k | 5: 200-280k | 3: 280-400k | 0: >400k with no invoiced engine rebuild (invoiced rebuild ≈ -150k)
- watch: very low km on an old dealer van (check historiapojazdu.gov.pl); UK imports in miles

## Engine & gearbox
- 10: proven engine, timing work invoiced, manual, >=130 KM (L2) / >=150 KM (L3-L4)
- 7: proven engine, 120-130 KM, timing history unknown
- 5: 110-120 KM in a heavy van; OM651 with no chain history
- 3: robotized manual (Quickshift, Comfort-Matic), 2.2 HDi Puma with no injector history, chip-tuned old engine, <110 KM
- 0: knock, smoke, oil loss, rattle on cold start
- watch: power in the registration (P.2) is kW; sellers write "95 KM" when it's 95 kW = 129 KM
- watch: "chip/mapa": ask whether the DPF or EGR was removed (illegal, MOT fail; verify)

## Weight (DMC) & payload
- Payload = DMC minus kerb weight (field G). 2 people + 100 l water + gear ≈ 350-450 kg.
- 10: >=700 kg | 7: 500-700 | 5: 400-500 | 3: 250-400 | 0: <250, or DMC >3,500 for a category B buyer
- watch: implausibly low kerb weights (a converted L2H2 is ~2,500-2,900 kg); ask for a weighbridge ticket. DMC 3,000 versions have little payload.

## Registered as camper
- 10: "samochód specjalny kempingowy" (M1), enough seats, swivel seats legal
- 7: kempingowy, fewer seats than needed
- 5: ciężarowy (N1), partition intact (legal as-is)
- 3: N1 with partition removed or swivel seats fitted (does not match the papers)
- 0: unregistered / build can't pass inspection
- N1→M1 costs ~11-19k PLN: post-modification inspection, PKD 45.20.Z workshop statement, homologated seats, excise 3.1% (<=2,000 cm3) or 18.6% (>2,000 cm3) on declared value (verify)
- M1 gives cheaper OC, a legal open cab, camper-only aires and sites, better resale. It does not change tolls or the licence needed (verify).
- watch: "nie dokonano przeróbek konstrukcyjnych" = deliberately kept N1

## Registered seats
- The number of seats in the registration document (dowód field S.1, includes the driver), not the seats fitted.
- 10: registered seats >= people travelling + 1 (a spare seat; better resale, families buy 4-seaters)
- 7: registered seats = people travelling
- 3: enough seats fitted, but registered for fewer than people travelling; or extra seats without belts
- 0: fewer registered seats than people travelling, with no realistic way to add them
- watch: seats fitted ≠ seats registered ("4 miejsca" in the text often means sleeping places, not seats); adding seats
  to the registration needs proper anchorage, belts and a post-modification inspection (verify); a swivel passenger
  seat on a double bench may reduce the registered count (verify)

## Conversion
Score only the evidence the listing shows; build quality is confirmed on site. Factory/OEM vs DIY is not a score by itself: a good DIY often has the better layout for living and working, a factory build is more predictable (seals, homologation, resale). A brand name alone (e.g. "Hymer") earns nothing beyond what the listing says about the build.
- 9: finished; branded parts named (Victron, Truma, Dometic, Seitz, MaxxFan); insulation type stated (Armaflex 10-19 mm, XPS floor); build photos or invoices. Or a named OEM model with its equipment listed.
- 7: finished; mostly branded parts; some build detail or photos
- 5: finished but few details, mixed or no-name parts; OEM with the model not stated
- 3: unfinished ("w 90%", "do dokończenia", parts "w kartonach")
- 0: none, or visibly unsafe in the photos (gas on hose clamps, loose wiring)
- 10: only after an on-site check, never from a listing
- watch: mineral wool or polystyrene insulation; "invested X PLN" is not a value
- on site (DIY especially): wiring and fusing, gas fittings, insulation behind a panel, moisture meter around every cut-out, ventilation

## Beds & layout
- 10: fixed bed >=190x140, standing height (H2+), dinette usable as a desk, swivel seats, garage
- 7: fixed bed 185x135-140, or a good convertible bed
- 5: transverse bed <=180 cm (Sprinter without flares), or 125 cm wide
- 3: bed made from seats nightly; H1 roof (no standing)
- 0: no real bed
- watch: Ducato/Jumper/Boxer are ~10 cm wider inside than a Sprinter

## Toilet & shower
- 10: wet room, indoor hot shower, cassette toilet, external shower
- 7: indoor shower tray or cassette toilet + external hot shower
- 5: portable toilet + external shower | 3: portable toilet only | 0: none
- watch: "prysznic" often means only an external hand shower

## Heating
- 10: branded, integrated with hot water (Truma Combi, wet Webasto)
- 7: branded diesel air heater (Webasto, Eberspächer, Autoterm)
- 5: unbranded Chinese diesel | 3: 230 V only | 0: none
- watch: "webasto" is used for any heater; "kupione, niezamontowane" = not installed

## Fridge & kitchen
- 10: 12 V compressor >=60 l (Dometic, Vitrifrigo, Indel), gas hob or induction on big lithium, sink
- 7: 12 V compressor 40-60 l
- 5: absorption 3-way (weak above ~32°C, fine on gas)
- 3: domestic 230 V fridge via inverter (drains battery)
- 0: none
- watch: induction on gel/AGM = campsite-only; "lodówka" with no type

## Solar & batteries
- 10: >=400 W, MPPT, LiFePO4 >=200 Ah, DC-DC >=30 A, shunt monitor
- 7: 250-400 W MPPT, LiFePO4 100-200 Ah or AGM >=200 Ah
- 5: 150-250 W, AGM/gel 150-200 Ah
- 3: <150 W or PWM, one ~100 Ah AGM
- 0: no leisure battery
- Usable: LiFePO4 ~90% of Ah, AGM/gel ~50%
- watch: 2-3 kW inverter on AGM; 230 V boiler/fridge/hob; LiFePO4 charged off the alternator without DC-DC; "solar" with no W; no-name LiFePO4 (a real 100 Ah weighs ~10-12 kg)

## Water tanks
- 10: fresh >=100 l, fixed grey >=70 l, gauges, outside filler
- 7: fresh 60-100 l, fixed grey | 5: fresh 40-60 l | 3: canisters 15-40 l, removable grey | 0: none
- watch: uninsulated underslung tanks freeze

## Gas & certificate
- 10: steel/copper 8-10 mm pipes with compression fittings, vented sealed locker, gas+CO detector, refillable LPG tank with EU adapters, leak-test protocol
- 7: steel pipes, standard bottle, detectors
- 5: no gas (all-electric or diesel): neutral
- 3: rubber hose on hose clamps ("cybanty"), bottle in the living space
- 0: gas smell, no shut-off
- PL: no mandatory periodic camper gas test (verify); DE: G 607 every 2 yrs (verify). PL 11 kg bottles can't be swapped in ES/PT/GR.

## Service history
- 10: invoices, one workshop/ASO, timing + injectors documented, dealer VIN history
- 7: partial invoices | 5: verbal ("serwisowany na bieżąco") | 3: none | 0: contradictions
- watch: ex-fleet = serviced but hard-used

## Rust & damp
- 10: clean underbody in photos, cavity wax, damp <15%
- 7: surface rust | 5: bubbling arches/doors/sills
- 3: holes at sills/jacking points, damp stains, fogged tail light (seal leak)
- 0: structural rot, soft floor, water ingress
- watch: vinyl wrap ("oklejony folią") or fresh underseal hiding rust; every roof/window cut-out is a leak risk

## Papers & ownership
- 10: PL plates, private owner 1+ yr, VIN matches, przegląd + OC valid, clean historiapojazdu
- 7: dealer, "faktura VAT marża", history report | 5: EU import, PL-registered
- 3: UK RHD "przekładka" or not yet registered | 0: no papers, seller not owner, active lease
- watch: przekładka quality (bulkhead rust, airbag light, steering play); private purchase = 2% PCC; net price = +23% VAT for a private buyer (verify)

## Base vehicles and engines
- Sprinter W906 (2006-2018), RWD, best resale: rust at arches, sills, doors, screen frame; ELV steering lock failure (stiff key)
  - OM646 2148 cm3 (to ~2009): front chain, robust. Prefer.
  - OM651 2143 cm3 (2009+): rear chain = engine out (~4-6.5k PLN); early Delphi injectors. Check chain stretch via Xentry camshaft deviation. 310 = 95 KM, 313 = 129 KM, 316 = 163 KM.
- Sprinter T1N W903 (to 2006): OM611/612/647 bulletproof, heavy rust
- Ducato/Jumper/Boxer X250 (2006-14) / X290 (2014+), FWD: rust at rear arches and sills; poor traction on wet grass with a heavy rear
  - 3.0 (Iveco F1C, 157-177 KM): durable. Prefer.
  - 2.3 MultiJet (F1A): good, timing belt (ask when last changed)
  - 2.2 HDi Puma: injectors and dual-mass flywheel, weak for a heavy camper
  - Comfort-Matic: avoid. 2016+ Euro 6 = AdBlue + DPF.
- Master III / Movano B / NV400 (2010+): 2.3 dCi M9T durable (500k+); Quickshift = actuator/clutch adaptation trouble (needs Renault Clip)
- Iveco Daily: ladder frame, RWD, very durable, harsh ride
- Transit (not sampled): Duratorq injectors, rust; 2.0 EcoBlue (2016+) wet belt (verify)
- Crafter 2006-16 (not sampled): W906 body, same rust; 2.0 TDI (2011+) preferred over 2.5 TDI (verify). Crafter/TGE 2017+ is a separate platform.

## Conversion makers
(Damp risk and build notes are for the on-site checks, not the score.)
- Premium OEM (Westfalia, Hymer/Malibu, La Strada): low damp risk if seals are maintained
- Mid OEM (Pössl, Globecar, Clever, Carado vans): check window and skylight seals
- Pro local builders (e.g. Najlah, Kraków): good parts; ask for the invoice and parts list
- Budget/semi-pro: Chinese heaters, 230 V appliances; medium damp risk
- DIY: judge by component brands, insulation, gas fittings, wiring and build photos; highest leak risk at cut-outs
- Pre-2005 timber-frame coachbuilts: high damp risk (none sampled)

- No coachbuilt prices sampled

## Red flags seen
- kW listed as KM; engine capacity that doesn't match the year; expired MOT in the text (reposted old ad)
- Very low km on an old dealer van; no VIN given
- Swivel seats or open cab on N1; 230 V fridge/boiler on AGM with a big inverter; gas on hose clamps
- Unfinished build priced as finished; "I invested X"; implausible kerb weight; damaged wrap; chip with no details; net price
- Scam patterns (general knowledge, not seen): 30%+ below market, seller abroad, deposit or courier before viewing, no viewing or VIN, reused photos

## Polish vocabulary
- zabudowa = conversion; kempingowy / samochód specjalny = M1 camper; ciężarowy/dostawczy = N1
- DMC = GVW; masa własna = kerb weight; ładowność = payload; ściana grodziowa = bulkhead; fotele obrotowe = swivel seats
- do negocjacji = negotiable; netto = +23% VAT; faktura VAT marża = dealer margin invoice; sprowadzony = imported; przekładka = RHD→LHD
- bezwypadkowy = claimed accident-free; rozrząd = timing; dwumasa = DMF; wtryski = injectors
- ogrzewanie postojowe = parking heater; bojler = water heater; przetwornica = inverter; szara woda = grey water
- przegląd = MOT; OC = liability insurance; zamiana = trade-in; pilne = urgent
