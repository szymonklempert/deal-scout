# Campervan check list

Used by the listing cards. Items marked (verify) are things I believe matter in Poland but have not checked against
current rules. Confirm them before relying on them.

## Paperwork and legal
- VIN on the listing, plate and first registration date, to look up the vehicle history on the official Polish service (historiapojazdu.gov.pl) (verify the exact inputs it asks for).
- Is the conversion registered as a camper in the registration document, with the body type changed? A van converted without registration can cause trouble at inspection and with insurance (verify).
- Payload: gross vehicle weight (DMC) minus empty weight. Many conversions leave little capacity for people, water and gear.
- Is the weight within what my licence allows (see criteria.md)?
- Seller is the registered owner; invoices or a purchase contract for the conversion work; service book.
- Import: origin country, whether the car was registered abroad, odometer plausibility.

## Base vehicle
- Known faults for the specific engine and gearbox of this model and year (check the model card when one exists).
- Rust: wheel arches, sills, underbody, door bottoms, window frames, floor edges.
- Service history: oil, timing belt/chain, gearbox oil, brakes. Diagnostic (OBD) scan; ask for a recent one.
- Tyres age and condition, suspension sag from the weight of the conversion.
- Test drive cold: starting, smoke, noises, gearbox judder, clutch, steering pull.

## Habitation area
- Damp and water ingress: smell, soft spots, stains around roof lights, windows and seams, behind cabinets and under the bed.
- Roof lights, windows and seals: cracking, discoloration.
- Fresh water and waste water: tanks, pump, taps, leaks; winter drain-down.
- Heating: type, age, tested running; exhaust routed safely.
- Insulation and ventilation: condensation signs, vents.

## Gas and electrical
- Gas system: fitted by a professional, date of the last inspection and certificate, hoses and regulator age, gas locker vented to the outside (verify the inspection rules).
- 12 V / 230 V: battery type and age, charger, inverter, fuse box, wiring quality, solar panel and controller, shore power inlet; no loose or amateur wiring.
- Appliances (fridge, hob, water heater) tested working.

## Layout and living
- Bed size and real standing and sitting height, storage, seating, kitchen, toilet or shower.
- Fits the people and trips in criteria.md.

## Listing red flags
- Price far below similar vans; urgent sale; refuses a viewing, a test drive or a diagnostic.
- Photos hide the roof, underbody or interior corners; very few photos; stock photos.
- Mileage low for the age while wear (pedals, seats, steering wheel) says otherwise.
- Seller asks for a deposit or payment before viewing; the car is "abroad" or "with a relative".
- Description and photos disagree; vague about conversion origin and papers.
